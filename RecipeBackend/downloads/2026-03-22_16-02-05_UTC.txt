Grandmas Wok Ep.1 - Spring Rolls

Ingredients
	•	½ lb fresh bean sprouts (or 1 lb canned, drained)
	•	½ lb raw shrimp, peeled, finely chopped
	•	½ lb lean ground pork
	•	3 tbsp oil (divided)
	•	4 cups finely chopped celery
	•	2–3 medium mushrooms, thinly sliced (~½ cup)
	•	1 tbsp soy sauce (plus a splash more for veg)
	•	1 tbsp Chinese rice wine or dry sherry
	•	½ tsp sugar
	•	2 tsp salt
	•	1 tbsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp chicken stock or water (slurry)
	•	Spring roll wrappers
	•	1 egg, beaten (for sealing)
	•	~3 cups neutral oil (for frying)

⸻

Recipe
	1. Rinse and thoroughly dry bean sprouts; prep all the vegetables and mix cornstarch slurry.
	2.	Heat wok over high heat, add 1 tbsp oil, cook ground pork for ~2 minutes until just losing pink.
	3.	Add shrimp and cook ~1 minute until just turning pink.
	4.	Add soy sauce, rice wine, and sugar; toss briefly, then remove pork and shrimp mixture and set aside.
	5.	Add remaining 2 tbsp oil to wok, cook mushrooms and celery for 4–5 minutes.
	6.	Add bean sprouts, salt, and a splash of soy sauce; toss and cook until slightly softened.
	7.	Return pork and shrimp to wok, mix everything together.
	8.	Add cornstarch slurry and stir until filling is lightly thickened and cohesive with minimal liquid.
	9.	Remove filling and let cool completely.
	10.	Place wrapper in diamond shape, add filling, fold bottom over, fold in sides, roll tightly, and seal edge with egg wash.
	11.	Heat oil to 350°F, fry egg rolls in batches for 3–4 minutes until golden and crispy.
	12.	Drain and serve immediately.

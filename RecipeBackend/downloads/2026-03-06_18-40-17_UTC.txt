Spicy Peanut Noodles 🍜 

Ingredients:
Noodles • Peanut butter • Chili crisp • Spring onions • Sesame seeds • Chili flakes • Garlic • Soy sauce • Rice vinegar • Lime juice • Neutral oil

Instructions:
Arrange peanut butter, chili crisp, spring onions, sesame seeds, chili flakes, garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, and lime juice on a plate. Cook noodles, reserve some water. Heat oil and pour over ingredients. Mix to create sauce, add noodles and reserved water, toss until coated. Garnish with spring onions and sesame seeds.
Bold, creamy, and ready in under 15 minutes!

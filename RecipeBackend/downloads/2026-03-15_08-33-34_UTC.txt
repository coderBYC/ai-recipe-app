Today’s bizarre creation: Seaweed Carbonara Ramen.
People often confuse Korean "Gim" and Japanese "Nori," but they are fundamentally different in production and texture. In Korea, having holes in the seaweed is common, but in Japan, sheets with holes are often considered sub-par. Korean Gim is a versatile multi-tasker, perfect for side dishes, while Nori is engineered for structure—specifically for things like maki or sushi. I’m using Nori here for its structural integrity.
